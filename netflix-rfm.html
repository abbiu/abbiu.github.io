Problem

A streaming platform needs to understand how different users engage with content so it can reduce churn and focus retention efforts where they matter most. Treating all users the same leads to wasted campaigns and missed opportunities, especially when some users binge, some drift, and others quietly disengage.

Approach

  • Defined RFM-style features: Recency (how recently a user watched), Frequency (how often), and a Monetary / Value proxy (engagement intensity or spend-related signal).
  • Standardized features and grouped users into interpretable segments using rule-based bins or clustering.
  • Labeled segments with clear, human-readable names to support decision-making.
  • Analyzed segment behavior to identify churn risk and engagement patterns.

What I contributed

  • Designed the segmentation framework and feature definitions from raw user-level data.
  • Balanced interpretability and analytical rigor so results could be used by non-technical stakeholders.
  • Translated segment outputs into concrete business actions (retention, win-back, onboarding).

Results & impact

The segmentation revealed distinct user groups with different engagement and churn risk profiles. It enabled targeted strategies such as win-back campaigns for at-risk users and nurture flows for new or growing users. The framework is reusable for subscription products or any business with repeat-behavior data.

Tech stack

Python or R (pandas/tidyverse), basic clustering or rule-based segmentation, Tableau (or similar) for visualization.
